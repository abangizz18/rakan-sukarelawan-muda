Who We Are

Driven by Youth, Focused on Change

Rakan Sukarelawan Muda was founded with a mission to bridge the gap between passionate youth and grassroot social causes. We believe every young person has the potential to build better communities.

Our Mission

To provide accessible volunteer opportunities that inspire personal growth and sustainable community development.We aim to create opportunities for young volunteers to develop important skills such as communication, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, creativity, and time management through real-world volunteer activities. Through our programmes and activities, we connect young people with community initiatives involving education, environmental awareness, social support, charity, and community development.

Our Vision

A society where young leaders actively drive positive social, educational, and environmental progress.We envision a future where every young person has the opportunity to participate, share ideas, develop their potential, and create meaningful positive change through volunteerism.

Core Values

Empathy, Action, Leadership, Transparency, and Community Empowerment in everything we do.These values encourage our volunteers to respect others, work together, take initiative, think creatively, act responsibly, and contribute towards creating positive and lasting changes in society.
